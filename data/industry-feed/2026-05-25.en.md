## 1. BW Epic Kosan expands use of GIT Coatings' biocide-free graphene coatings
- Date: 2026-05-28
- Category: Company
- Summary: Five LPG carriers have been coated so far (with one more in dock). On the case vessels, average hull roughness after coating was 60–70 μm and out-of-dock power improved by about 6% versus the old coating; combined with proactive cleaning by onboard robots, no hard fouling was seen for over a year; the first vessel using the new-generation XGIT-FORCE had an average roughness of 38 μm. The above is as reported by the manufacturer and the shipowner.
- Source: https://bw-group.com/newsroom/articles/2026/05/bw-epic-kosan-expands-use-of-git-coatings-following-6-out-of-dock-fuel-savings-and-maintained-hull-performance/
