## 1. IMO MEPC 84 agrees to manage ship biofouling through a standalone mandatory instrument
- Date: 2026-05-01
- Category: Regulation
- Summary: MEPC 84 (27 April to 1 May, London) agreed to develop a standalone, legally binding instrument for the control and management of ships' biofouling to minimise the transfer of invasive aquatic species, with follow-up work assigned to the PPR Sub-Committee.
- Source: https://www.imo.org/en/mediacentre/pressbriefings/pages/imo-progresses-work-on-ship-emissions-pollution-and-ocean-protection.aspx
- Relevance: High (a mandatory convention will directly affect antifouling coating selection, inspection and cleaning requirements, and compliance costs on every route)

## 2. HullPIC 2026: industry calls for greater transparency in hull performance data
- Date: 2026-04-28
- Category: Company
- Summary: The 11th HullPIC was held in Italy with more than 90 shipowners, service providers, coating manufacturers and experts attending; participants including Jotun argued that linking hull performance measurement with biofouling management is a core industry challenge, and that standardised, comparable performance claims based on methods such as ISO 19030 are needed.
- Source: https://www.jotun.com/ww-en/news-and-insights/jotun-insider/a-call-for-transparency-in-hull-performance
- Relevance: Medium (once performance claim conventions are unified, cross-comparisons between commercial coatings will become more reliable)

## 3. 8th Marine Fouling Protection Technology Symposium held in Xiamen
- Date: 2026-04-28
- Category: Technology
- Summary: The Marine Fouling Protection Technology Committee of the Chinese Society for Corrosion and Protection held the symposium in Xiamen on 17–19 April, with more than 200 participants, 16 plenary talks and 48 parallel-session talks covering the engineering of antifouling coatings, molecular design of new antifouling materials, intelligent protection, and test and evaluation methods; the 9th edition is scheduled for 2027, hosted by Harbin Engineering University.
- Source: https://www.cscp.org.cn/site/content/3224.html
- Relevance: Medium (reflects the overall direction of domestic antifouling formulation research and evaluation methods)
