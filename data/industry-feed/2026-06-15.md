## 1. IMO holds in-water cleaning workshop in Fiji to help develop national regulatory schemes
- Date: 2026-06-17
- Category: Regulation
- Summary: On 2–3 June, Fijian government departments and 14 private-sector organisations met in Lami Bay to discuss how to implement the IMO in-water cleaning guidelines (MEPC.1/Circ.918); topics included assessing cleaning impact by sailing profile and by coating type, condition and age, whether capture should be required, and how to verify effectiveness; the workshop was part of the IMO–Norad TEST Biofouling project.
- Source: https://www.imo.org/en/mediacentre/pages/whatsnew-2459.aspx
- Relevance: Medium (Pacific island states are beginning to establish cleaning regulation, and coating type and condition will be factored into cleaning approval considerations)

## 2. Chalmers study: among yacht antifouling paints compared, biocide-free silicone performs best
- Date: 2026-06-16
- Category: Technology
- Summary: In panel tests of up to 6 months at sites in Sweden, Denmark and France, silicone biocide-free coatings gave the best antifouling performance; coatings containing tralopyril and zinc pyrithione came next but released far more than acceptable levels; five coatings with cuprous oxide contents of 6–32 wt% performed similarly, and low copper was equally effective. The paper was published in the Journal of Hazardous Materials.
- Source: https://www.chalmers.se/en/current/news/env-biocide-free-antifouling-paint-performs-best-against-fouling/
- Relevance: High (directly supports formulation decisions to lower copper content and select FRC, and flags the environmental risk of tralopyril)

## 3. University of South Carolina receives ONR funding to design zwitterionic antifouling materials with computational simulation
- Date: 2026-06-17
- Category: Technology
- Summary: Tao Wei's team began a new project in May, using multiscale simulation to study the hydration layer at material surfaces and to design zwitterionic antifouling polymer coatings that can adapt to both static and underway conditions, and to different salinities and acidities.
- Source: https://sc.edu/study/colleges_schools/engineering_and_computing/news_events/news/2026/tao_wei_marine_fouling_resistant_materials.php
- Relevance: Low (still at the fundamental research stage, with no near-term impact on formulation selection)

## Weekly takeaway
Panel evidence from multiple European sea areas shows that biocide-free silicone performs best and low-copper formulations are also effective enough, while some "environmentally friendly" biocides are in fact more toxic. Through capacity-building projects, the IMO is promoting the establishment of in-water cleaning regulation in developing countries.
