## 2. Cerium oxide nanoparticles as a non-toxic co-biocide improve self-polishing antifouling paints
- Date: 2026-09-15
- Category: Technology
- Summary: Adding 0.1–0.5 wt% CeO₂ to an SPC formulation improved antibacterial activity, surface hydrophilicity and copper release, with the potential to reduce copper use.
- Source: https://www.european-coatings.com/news/coatings-technologies/cerium-oxide-improves-biocide-release-in-antifouling-paint/
- Relevance: High (directly concerns co-biocide choice and dosage in copper-based SPC formulations)

## 4. PLOS ONE: ZnO nanorod antifouling coating on micropatterned polymers
- Date: 2026-09-18
- Category: Technology
- Summary: A team at Sultan Qaboos University grew ZnO nanorods on three micropatterned polymer surfaces, achieving contact angles of about 150–165°; under laboratory flow conditions the coating reduced attachment of E. coli and Amphora diatoms, and acute toxicity was assessed using whiteleg shrimp larvae; sea trials are still needed.
- Source: https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0357826
- Relevance: Medium (a low-toxicity antifouling approach combining micro/nano structure with inorganic nanomaterials)
