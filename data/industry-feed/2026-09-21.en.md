## 2. ACS AMI: piezoelectric antifouling coating inspired by the electroactive skin of the African clawed frog
- Date: 2026-09-24
- Category: Technology
- Summary: A team including South China University of Technology combined a graphene-enhanced PVDF piezoelectric matrix with a ZIF-8-encapsulated, photoresponsive NO donor; under mechanical stimulation from water flow it redistributes surface charge to promote fouling detachment, while releasing NO in a controlled manner (per the Crossref abstract).
- Source: https://doi.org/10.1021/acsami.6c15218
- Relevance: Medium (a multi-mechanism piezoelectric + controlled-release design, still at the laboratory stage)

## 3. Chalmers study in Sweden: biocide-free silicone antifouling paint performs best in yacht trials
- Date: 2026-09-25
- Category: Technology
- Summary: The study compared seven antifouling paints on the coasts of Sweden, Denmark and France, and the biocide-free silicone paint performed best; a product marketed as eco-friendly that contains tralopyril proved highly toxic; low-copper paints can be as effective as high-copper paints.
- Source: https://www.boatingnz.co.nz/2026/09/fouling-study-biocide-free-performs-best/
- Relevance: High (directly supports copper reduction and silicone biocide-free formulation choices)
