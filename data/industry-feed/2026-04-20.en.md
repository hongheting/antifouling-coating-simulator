## 1. Singapore's MPA selects six partners for in-port underwater hull inspection and cleaning innovation trials
- Date: 2026-04-22
- Category: Regulation
- Summary: The MPA selected Alicia Bots, C-Leanship, Neptune Robotics, Oceanis Robotics, RINA and SEAHI Robotics from 19 proposals submitted by 36 companies, with nearly S$3.7 million in R&D co-funding from the MINT fund; the trials will run for 18 months from the second half of 2026, focusing on complex areas such as propellers, rudders and sea chests, operations in strong currents, and debris collection requirements.
- Source: https://www.mpa.gov.sg/media-centre/details/mpa-selects-six-partners-for-in-water-hull-inspection-and-cleaning-innovation-trials-in-the-port-of-singapore
- Relevance: Medium (Singapore is a hub for high-fouling Southeast Asian routes; wider adoption of in-port robotic cleaning will raise requirements on coating cleanability)

## 2. Neptune Robotics invests US$12 million in a Singapore manufacturing and R&D base
- Date: 2026-04-20
- Category: Company
- Summary: The company plans to expand its autonomous hull cleaning capacity fivefold, reaching 60 cleanings per day by 2027; the new base focuses on computer vision and machine learning, aiming for round-the-clock operation in low visibility and currents of up to 4 knots, with a service network currently covering 61 ports in Singapore and China.
- Source: https://smartmaritimenetwork.com/2026/04/20/neptune-robotics-invests-12m-in-singapore-facility-to-expand-autonomous-hull-cleaning/
- Relevance: Low (expansion of a cleaning service provider; only an indirect influence on formulation choices)

## 3. Kawasaki Kisen tug BISAN MARU launched, becoming Japan's first tug fitted with an ultrasonic hull antifouling system
- Date: 2026-04-22
- Category: Product
- Summary: Ordered by SEAGATE, the vessel is fitted with both a biofuel blending unit and an ultrasonic antifouling system from M-SAT, which uses ultrasound to suppress marine growth; the system was originally developed for a hybrid-electric tug to be delivered in 2027 and is being validated in service on this vessel first, with entry into service at Mizushima Port expected in August 2026.
- Source: https://www.kline.co.jp/en/news/logistics/logistics-20260422.html
- Relevance: Low (a non-coating supplementary antifouling method, currently only being validated on a harbour tug)
