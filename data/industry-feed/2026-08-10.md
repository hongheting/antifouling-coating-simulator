## 1. ACS Applied Polymer Materials: hydrolytic self-polishing antifouling coating with pH-responsive arbutin release
- Date: 2026-08-10
- Category: Technology
- Summary: A Dalian Maritime University team used a PDMS–polyurethane acrylate as a hydrolysable matrix, loaded with arbutin-bearing ZIF-8 and nano-SiO₂ for responsive release and reinforcement; 60-day mass loss 0.6 mg/cm², adhesion 5B, shear strength 14.1 MPa (per the Crossref abstract).
- Source: https://doi.org/10.1021/acsapm.6c02995
- Relevance: High (a formulation combination of natural antifoulant + self-polishing matrix that can be drawn on directly)

## 3. ACS AMI: South China University of Technology slow-curing polyurea peelable antifouling coating
- Date: 2026-08-12
- Category: Technology
- Summary: Using secondary-amine oligosiloxane nanoclusters as the crosslinker and introducing a reactive amphiphilic polymer with fluorine and PEG side chains, which migrates to the surface on drying to inhibit fouling; adhesion of 2–8 MPa on substrates including steel, ceramic, copper and glass, and peelable for easy maintenance (per the Crossref abstract).
- Source: https://doi.org/10.1021/acsami.6c12686
- Relevance: Medium (amphiphilic surface migration and peelable design suit equipment / temporary-protection scenarios)
