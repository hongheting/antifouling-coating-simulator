## 7. Shanghai Ocean University and others reveal polar bacterial adhesin YadA drives both corrosion and biofouling
- Date: 2026-08-29
- Category: Technology
- Summary: The study was published online in Corrosion Science: the polar psychrophilic bacterium secretes far more extracellular protein than temperate bacteria, which can aggravate low-temperature steel corrosion and more strongly induce attachment and metamorphosis of macrofouling organisms, providing a basis for adhesin-targeted antifouling design.
- Source: https://www.chinanews.com.cn/gn/2026/08-29/10686556.shtml
