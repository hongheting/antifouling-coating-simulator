## 3. Fremantle Port in-water cleaning pilot passes six months and is seen as a replicable template
- Date: 2026-09-30
- Category: Regulation
- Summary: Australia's first arrangement permitting controlled in-water cleaning of commercial vessels in port; before cleaning, invasive-species and coating-chemistry risks are assessed, and the wash water is captured, filtered to 10 μm and UV-treated before discharge; the pilot runs for three years with annual reviews.
- Source: https://www.thedcn.com.au/news/biosecurity-keep-it-clean
- Relevance: Medium (relevant to cleanability on Australian routes and to coating-chemistry risk assessment)
