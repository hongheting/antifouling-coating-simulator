## 1. Norwegian study: eDNA detects 19 alien species in material recovered from robot hull cleaning
- Date: 2026-07-01
- Category: Technology
- Summary: NINA and the Norwegian Institute of Marine Research analysed recovered material and wash water from 9 vessels cleaned by ECOsubsea robots at Tananger and Bergen, detecting 19 alien species not yet established in Norway, 8 of them high-risk "doorstep species", with an international cruise ship carrying the most; these species were not detected in port water samples.
- Source: https://www.nina.no/en/news/all-articles-and-news/ship-hull-cleaning-reveals-alien-species-on-their-way-to-norway
- Relevance: Medium (cleaning with capture can be used for biosecurity monitoring, and requirements for capture cleaning on Nordic routes may be further strengthened)
