## 1. Fleet Robotics magnetic-adhesion stepping hull-cleaning robot (preventive maintenance at sea)
- Date: 2026-10-14
- Category: Technology
- Summary: A magnetic-foot stepping cleaning robot can be permanently carried aboard a vessel, brush the hull autonomously at sea and return to its dock to recharge; it has been tested on a real vessel at WHOI. The report cautions that overly frequent cleaning may damage coatings.
- Source: https://news.northeastern.edu/2026/10/14/ship-hull-cleaning-robot-co-op/
- Relevance: Medium (affects how cleanable foul-release / silicone coatings fit active-cleaning routes, and their abrasion-resistance design)

## 3. Biofouling: dual-site field sea trial (Florida estuary and ocean) of UVC combined with marine coatings
- Date: 2026-10-05
- Category: Technology
- Summary: Fouling was heavier in the turbid estuary and turbidity attenuated the UVC; at the ocean site, 9-month panels showed UVC could suppress macrofouling on four coatings; silicone was the most chemically sensitive to UVC, but the overall impact was limited.
- Source: https://doi.org/10.1080/08927014.2026.2736784
- Relevance: High (directly relevant to UV-C / coating synergistic antifouling, turbidity zoning and duty-cycle design)

## 4. POC: hydrodynamic stability window of a xanthan-gum hydrogel coating and field copper reduction
- Date: 2026-10-13
- Category: Technology
- Summary: OCT + CFD converted rotor shear into an equivalent ship speed; the hydrogel thinned gradually at an equivalent speed of about 7 knots; xanthan gum with particle size <32 μm could reduce Cu₂O from 10 wt% to 6 wt% while achieving comparable field antifouling performance.
- Source: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.porgcoat.2026.110676
- Relevance: High (low-copper self-polishing formulations and hydrogel-layer stability within the service-speed window)

## 5. POC: GARField NMR non-destructively characterises CuPy release in PDMS
- Date: 2026-10-09
- Category: Technology
- Summary: 1D ¹H NMR profiles tracked the diffusion-controlled release front of CuPy in opaque antifouling coatings, cross-checked against SEM; reliable detection is possible above about 15 wt%, making the method suitable for formulation screening.
- Source: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.porgcoat.2026.110674
- Relevance: Medium (a release-kinetics characterisation method that can be borrowed for evaluating biocide / functional-component release)

## Weekly takeaway
On the cleaning side, the trend continues toward shipboard, at-sea preventive robots, with emphasis on coating compatibility. UVC–coating synergy, hydrogel-enabled copper reduction and NMR release characterisation appeared in the same week, serving low-biocide approaches and coating-plus-active-antifouling combinations. Domestically, the new-quality-productive-forces policy, the ship antifouling operations rules and the carbon-footprint chapter of the climate annual report advance in parallel, while US copper label rules keep tightening — eco-friendly formulations, pilot-scale work and carbon-footprint data become the threshold for the next stage.
