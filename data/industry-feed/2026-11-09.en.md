## 1. Cargill × Neptune Robotics robotic hull-cleaning trial in Singapore
- Date: 2026-11-02
- Category: Technology
- Summary: Cargill and Neptune Robotics have launched an 18-month collaboration under the Maritime and Port Authority of Singapore (MPA) innovation programme for underwater hull inspection and cleaning, using an AI remotely operated cleaning robot (the flagship MAGNETO) to clean and inspect Cargill's calling vessels underwater, with before-and-after performance comparisons to calculate fuel savings; the company states its system can clean a fully laden Capesize vessel within 24 hours and covers 61 ports in Singapore and China; the fuel-saving results have not yet been published and remain to be measured in practice.

## 2. EU member states support non-renewal of the Selektope (medetomidine) approval
- Date: 2026-11-03
- Category: Regulation
- Summary: EU member states voted to support the European Commission's proposal not to renew the approval of Selektope (active substance medetomidine) as a PT21 antifouling biocide; once the formal decision is published, a transition period of about 180 days is expected, during which Selektope-containing coatings may still be supplied and applied in the EU, after which they may no longer be placed on the EU market; vessels already coated are unaffected. I-Tech states that about 95% of the global commercial fleet can continue to use it, that its direct EU exposure is about 4% of its revenue, and that it will object through scientific, regulatory and legal channels.
- Source: https://storage.mfn.se/0cbf4c2d-555a-4994-ba5f-4ff032888d23/eu-member-states-support-non-renewal-of-selektope-approval-limited-commercial-impact-expected.pdf
- Relevance: High (directly affects EU market access for Selektope-containing commercial antifouling paints, favouring tracking of biocide-free routes)
- Note: Pending review (EU market-access wording for Selektope-containing products such as CMP Sea Premier in the shortlist / commercial-products.md; ratings unchanged)

## 5. Southern California SCAQMD proposes amending Rule 1106 to tighten VOC and siloxanes in marine coatings
- Date: 2026-11-06
- Category: Regulation
- Summary: The South Coast Air Quality Management District (SCAQMD) in Southern California has issued a CEQA exemption notice for amending Rule 1106 on marine and pleasure-craft coatings: it proposes phasing out coatings containing more than trace amounts of parachlorobenzotrifluoride (pCBtF) and tert-butyl acetate (t-BAc), prohibiting the sale and use of coatings containing more than trace amounts of fully methylated siloxanes, and adding VOC limits together with record-keeping and labelling requirements; the expected reduction is 23.9 pounds of VOC per day.
- Source: https://ceqanet.lci.ca.gov/2026110264
- Relevance: Medium (methylated-siloxane restrictions directly touch silicone foul-release coating formulations, and tighter VOC rules affect solvent-borne systems)

## 8. Paper on a bio-based amphiphilic polymer antifouling coating published
- Date: 2026-11
- Category: Technology
- Summary: The November 2026 issue of Marine Pollution Bulletin (vol. 232, article 120083) published a study of a bio-based amphiphilic polymer antifouling coating: the coating was prepared from renewable platform chemicals by free-radical polymerisation that avoids harmful solvents, tuning the hydrophilic/hydrophobic balance; in laboratory tests, the hydrophobe-dominant formulation inhibited diatom attachment by more than 90% and showed no detectable acute toxicity to Artemia; however, in static real-sea panel immersion the amphiphilic formulations failed to prevent fouling accumulation, indicating that further optimisation is still needed. Only the publication month (November 2026) is known; the exact online date has not been announced.
- Source: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.marpolbul.2026.120083
- Relevance: Medium (design ideas for copper-free bio-based antifouling binders, benchmarkable against biocide-free formulation routes; the shortfall in real-sea performance is an important caveat)

## Weekly takeaway
Robotic cleaning has entered the real-ship validation stage with major shipowners: the Cargill–Neptune Singapore trial ties cleaning efficiency, coating compatibility and measured fuel savings together. The EU non-renewal of Selektope and California's siloxane restrictions tighten, on two fronts, the compliance space for biocide-containing and silicone routes; the copper-free bio-based coating paper shows there is still a gap between laboratory algae inhibition and real-sea performance. Leading manufacturers' marine coating volumes continue to grow and domestic package certifications expand — the next stage will look more at measured cleaning data, regional market-access changes for products containing active substances, and real-sea evidence for copper-free formulations.
