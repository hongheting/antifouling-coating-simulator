## 1. South China University of Technology team led by Guangzhao Zhang achieves scaled-up application of controllably degradable antifouling coatings
- Date: 2026-07-29
- Category: Technology
- Summary: The coatings are based on controllably degradable polymers such as polyester–polyurethane and polyester–polyacrylate; hydrolysis of ester groups in seawater drives layer-by-layer degradation and dynamic surface renewal to inhibit attachment, without relying on traditional toxic biocides. They have been applied on COSCO Shipping vessels and in scenarios including wave-energy platforms, subsea data centres, and marine ranches.
- Source: https://www.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202607/29/WS6a6954daa310986e2b467dbb.html
- Relevance: High (degradable self-renewing resins are one of the core routes for low-biocide / biocide-free formulations)

## 2. Marine Chemical Research Institute silicone antifouling coating used on a 300,000-tonne VLCC for the first time
- Date: 2026-07-31
- Category: Product
- Summary: The VLCC "Island Splendor" left dock on 30 July after completing dry-docking in Zhoushan; the report says this is the first time a domestically produced high-performance marine coating has been used on a VLCC. The in-service estimates cited in the article — speed loss of 1.5%–2% and fuel savings of 11%–13% — are company figures and have not been independently verified.
- Source: https://www.27580.cn/important/198634.html
- Relevance: High (a domestic low-surface-energy silicone system entering large tanker service can serve as a fouling-release candidate reference)
