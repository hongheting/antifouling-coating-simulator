## 1. PPG SIGMAGLIDE 2390 receives the American Chemical Society Green Chemistry Challenge Award
- Date: 2026-07-06
- Category: Product
- Summary: This biocide-free silicone fouling-release coating won in the "Designing Greener Chemicals" category; it uses HYDRORESET technology, in which the surface rearranges at the nanoscale on contact with water to form an ultra-smooth, low-friction interface, and it can be applied by electrostatic spraying. PPG states that third-party assessment to ISO 19030/ITTC showed power savings of up to 20%.
