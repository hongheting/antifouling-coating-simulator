## 1. UMCES leads 24 scientists in warning that vessels stranded in the Strait of Hormuz could trigger a bioinvasion "super-spreader" event
- Date: 2026-07-22
- Category: Technology
- Summary: The paper was published in Biological Invasions: since the strait closed on 2026-02-28, large numbers of merchant vessels have been anchored for long periods in the Gulf, with bacteria, algae, barnacles and other organisms rapidly attaching to their hulls; once the vessels resume sailing they may carry invasive species to ports worldwide, and the authors recommend cleaning and assessment before departure, among other measures.

- Relevance: Medium (long anchorage / low-speed conditions highlight static antifouling capability, a useful reference for coating selection on tropical Gulf routes)
