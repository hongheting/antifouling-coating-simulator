## 1. PortPIC conference review: as the IMO convention nears, in-water cleaning enters the "verifiable" stage
- Date: 2026-10-12
- Category: Regulation
- Summary: Capture is expected to be written into future rules, but the capture proportion, particle size, measurement approach and verification method are still undecided; a pilot at the Port of Bergen with DNV and NIVA proposed a benchmark of ≥80% capture and separation efficiency, and the BRADD project will provide data to IMO PPR by 2027.
- Source: https://www.cleanshippinginternational.com/from-clean-to-proven-in-water-cleaning-faces-its-verification-test/
- Relevance: Medium (affects how foul-release/silicone coatings fit routes requiring in-water cleaning, and what cleaning demands of coatings)

## 7. Biointerphases: bacteria in early marine biofilms are generally extremely copper-tolerant
- Date: 2026-10-13
- Category: Technology
- Summary: After two weeks of panel immersion in Chesapeake Bay, the bacteria on uncoated control panels were as copper-tolerant as those on copper-coated panels, able to grow at copper concentrations above 70 mg/L (the EPA short-term seawater limit is 18 μg/L).
- Source: https://publishing.aip.org/publications/latest-content/bacteria-dna-has-evolved-to-withstand-copper/
- Relevance: High (directly concerns the long-term effectiveness of copper-based antifoulants against microbial films)
- Note: Pending review (evidence basis for the copper-based SPC shortlist; rating unchanged)

## 8. Chemical Engineering Journal: light-responsive microcapsule on-demand antifouling coating inspired by fluorescent corals
- Date: 2026-10-17
- Category: Technology
- Summary: Microcapsules of eugenol, SiO₂/TiO₂ and long-afterglow phosphor are embedded in polyurea-modified PDMS, releasing faster under strong light and losing less under weak light; reported antibacterial rates of 98–99%, anti-diatom rates of 75–80% and a thermal self-healing efficiency of 93.3%.
- Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1385894726106603
- Relevance: Medium (stimulus-responsive release and the PDMS system are of reference value for formulation design, and relate to routes differentiated by light intensity)

## Weekly takeaway
IMO convention discussion is shifting from "whether to capture" to "how to measure and how to prove it", and China is likewise developing a group standard for accelerated assessment methods for long-life antifouling coatings. The biocide-free route advanced on both the product and technology-licensing fronts. New evidence that bacteria are generally copper-tolerant weakens the long-term outlook for copper-based antifoulants, and research into stimulus-responsive, low-release formulations continued to heat up.
