## 1. Brazil's NORMAM-401 biofouling rules take effect on 10 June, with penalties deferred to January 2028
- Date: 2026-06-02
- Category: Regulation
- Summary: The rules align with IMO MEPC.207(62) and require ships to carry a Biofouling Management Plan and Record Book; only microfouling is permitted on the hull, cleaning is required only at macrofouling of level 2 or above and needs prior approval; violations carry fines of up to R$2 million and possible detention, but enforcement of penalties is not expected until January 2028.
- Source: https://www.iss-shipping.com/update-brazil-biofouling-regulations-effective-june-2026/
- Relevance: High (directly affects antifouling coating selection and cleaning planning on South American routes)
