## Weekly takeaway
On the cleaning side, the emphasis is on auditable climate footprints and zero untreated discharge of cleaning wastewater; copper-free / biocide-free products advance in parallel with academic progress on covalent QSI binders. Domestically, carbon-footprint national standards put ships on the list, and MIIT's dual control plus the science–industry integration policy layer on pilot-scale work and commercialisation evaluation — the next stage will look more at carbon-footprint data, cleaning compliance proof and verifiable evidence for copper-free formulations.
